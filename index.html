Why a négociant gets "capped"

Louis Jadot can be a genuinely top producer — legendary winemaking, a full Grand Cru range, and it owns serious vineyards too. But in Burgundy's prestige and price hierarchy it stays a rung below the cult estates, and that ceiling is set by the model, not the wine in the glass. Collectors pay the real premium for tiny, grower-controlled, vine-to-bottle estates. A négociant — even one buying immaculate fruit and bottling 80%+ of it itself — reads as higher-volume and less rare, so its prestige tops out below the domaine apex. The honest nuance: a top négociant like PYCM or Maison Leroy can match a great domaine in the glass; what it can't do is climb onto DRC's pedestal. Note how blurry the line is — PYCM, Castagnier, Faiveley and Jadot all own vineyards and buy fruit (marked H).
